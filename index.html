Our Mission

Founded in 2021 in the heart of Vientiane, TheSale3 was born from a passion for the untamed beauty of Laos. We believe that every trail has a story, and every campsite is a home waiting to be built.

We don't just sell gear; we equip expeditions. From the rainy mountains of Phou Khoun to the misty rivers of Vang Vieng, our equipment is rigorously tested to ensure you stay safe, dry, and inspired in the wild.
